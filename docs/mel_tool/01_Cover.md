**Subtitle:** Sustainable Aquaculture for Women Advancement (SAWA)  |  Programme management firm: Agri-Impact Ltd. (AIL)  |  Year 1: July 2026 to June 2027

Version v3  |  18 October 2026  |  MEL focal person: Muhammadu Sungsong Aboubakr, MEAL Officer


## Purpose

This workbook is CEL's MEL system for SAWA. It shows what CEL will deliver (framework and theory of change), how data moves from participants to Agri-Impact (data flow and roles), what has been achieved against targets (IPTT), and what happens in Quarter 2 (onboarding and training schedules). The sheets are grouped so that CEL's programme team can find the input sheets and schedules quickly, and Agri-Impact can read the framework, reporting tables and definitions in the order of its own template.

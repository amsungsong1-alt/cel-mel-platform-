## 1. Number of young women in Youth-in-Work (YiW) attributable to program interventions across the aquaculture value chain (all aquaculture-related work)

**Definition & Description:**

Definition: Number of young women aged 18–35 who are engaged in income-earning wage employment or self-employment attributable to programme interventions across the aquaculture value chain. A participant is counted as YiW only when she/he is actually engaged in work and earning an income.

Youth-in-Work (YiW): refers to young people who have gained new, additional, or improved employment, either
through self-employment or wage employment, as a direct result of Foundation support. This includes:
• New work: First-time or re-entry into work after unemployment.
• Additional work: A second income-generating activity alongside existing work.
• Improved work: Enhanced quality or conditions of work (e.g., higher income, stability, respect, or purpose).

A participant should only be counted as YiW once there is evidence that she is actually engaged in work and earning income, rather than merely receiving training or programme support.

**Disaggregation:**

Disability status: PWD/Non-PWD
Employment type: Wage/Self
Pathway: input supply, production, processing, marketing, logistics, digital services, etc.
Location: region, district, community

**Unit of Measurement:**

Number

**Data Type:**

Integer

**Data Collection method:**

Participant surveys/interviews
review of YiW and programme records

**Data Source:**

YiW records

Programme records

Participant surveys/assessments

**Frequency:**

According to schedule


## 2. Percent of young women in YiW who access dignifed and fulfilling (D&F) work attributable to program interventions

**Definition & Description:**

Definition: Percentage of young women already classified as YiW whose work meets the programme criteria for dignified and fulfilling work.

Dignified and Fulfilling (D&F) work: As defined by the young people, youth are considered to be accessing dignified and fulfilling work if they have reliable income and at least one of the other three markers: reputable work, workplace respect, or sense of purpose.
• Reliable income: Provides enough income for the youth and their dependents
• Reputable work: Your work is respected by the people you care about
• Respect in the workplace: Youth feel respected in their workplaces
• Sense of purpose: Gives the youth a sense of purpose.

Only young women already classified as YiW are included in the denominator for this indicator.

**Disaggregation:**

Disability status
Employment type
Aquaculture pathway
Location

**Unit of Measurement:**

Percentage

**Data Type:**

Decimal

**Data Collection method:**

Participant surveys/assessments
review of YiW records

**Data Source:**

YiW records

Programme and participant surveys/assessments

**Frequency:**

According to schedule

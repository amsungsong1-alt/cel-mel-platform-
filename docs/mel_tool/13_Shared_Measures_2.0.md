## 13. SM 5 – Foundation-supported youth accessing new, additional, or improved work (wage or self-employment) - SM 5.1 – Number of employed youth (wage and self-employment) - 13. Number of young women in Youth-in-Work (YiW) attributable to program interventions across the aquaculture value chain (all aquaculture-related work)

**Definition & Description:**

Definition: Number of young women aged 15–35 who are engaged in income-earning wage employment or self-employment attributable to programme interventions across the aquaculture value chain. A participant is counted as YiW only when she is actually engaged in work and earning an income.

Youth-in-Work (YiW): refers to young women who have transitioned into income-earning wage employment or self-employment in aquaculture-related work.

A participant should only be counted as YiW once there is evidence that she is actually engaged in work and earning income, rather than merely receiving training or programme support.

**Disaggregation:**

Disability status: PWD/Non-PWD
Youth category: 15–24, 25–35
Employment type: Wage/Self
Pathway: input supply, production, processing, marketing, logistics, digital services, etc.
Location: region, district, community

**Unit of Measurement:**

Number

**Data Type:**

Integer

**Data Collection method:**

Participant surveys/interviews
review of YiW and programme records

**Data Source:**

YiW records

Programme records

Participant surveys/assessments

According to schedule

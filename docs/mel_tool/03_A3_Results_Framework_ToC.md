## 1. Theory of change


| Goal | Over 60,000 financially disadvantage young women and persons with disabilities (PWD) accessing D&F work across the aquaculture value chain, resulting in economic empowerment, resilience and poverty reduction at individual and community levels |
|---|---|


| Logic | If young women and persons with disabilities (PWDs) build skills and confidence (capacity building) and gain access to markets, finance and a supportive network (market linkage), then they can start or grow enterprises and take up work in the aquaculture value chain. If that work is dignified and fulfilling, they earn reliable income, which strengthens their economic empowerment, resilience and well-being. This only holds where workplaces are inclusive and safe, and where participants can be traced and verified. |
|---|---|


| Pathways to change | Capacity building: participants gain confidence and demonstrate improved capacity. | Market linkage: participants gain increased access to markets, finance and opportunities. |
|---|---|---|

## 3. If-then logic

•  If young women and PWDs are mobilised, onboarded with valid IDs and complete the BDS and gender trainings, then they gain the skills and confidence to start or grow enterprises.

•  If enterprises are prepared and referred, and TechnoServe funds them, then they can expand and create work, leading to more young women and PWDs in dignified and fulfilling work.

•  If WAN provides mentoring, market access, advocacy and leadership, then members gain agency and voice, which sustains enterprise growth and resilience.

•  If workplaces and partners adopt inclusion and safeguarding standards, then PWDs and young women can take up and keep work safely.
